What Should I Check First When My AC Isn't Cooling Properly?

When the Texas heat is relentless, a malfunctioning AC can quickly become a homeowner's nightmare. If your air conditioner isn't cooling properly, there are a few things you can check before reaching out to a professional. Here's your step-by-step guide to troubleshooting AC issues.

Check the Thermostat Settings

First, ensure that your thermostat is set to 'cool' and the temperature is set lower than the current room temperature. Sometimes, simple errors in settings can cause your AC to run improperly. If the thermostat is battery-operated, replacing the batteries could also solve the problem.

Inspect the Air Filter

A clogged air filter can impede airflow, causing your AC to work harder and less efficiently. Check your filter and replace it if it's dirty. During the hotter months, it's a good idea to replace or clean filters every one to three months.

Examine the Circuit Breakers

Sometimes, the issue might be as simple as a tripped circuit breaker. Check your home's electrical panel and reset any tripped breakers. If the breaker trips again, it might indicate a larger electrical issue that requires professional attention.

Check for Obstructions

Inspect the outdoor unit for any obstructions like leaves, debris, or tall grass that could be blocking airflow. Clear any obstacles around the unit and ensure that it has at least two feet of clearance on all sides.

Look for Refrigerant Leaks

Low refrigerant levels can significantly impair your AC's cooling efficiency. If you suspect a refrigerant leak—indicated by ice buildup on the unit or hissing sounds—it's crucial to contact a local Hill Country HVAC professional. Handling refrigerant requires specialized equipment and expertise.

When to Call a Professional

If you've gone through these steps and your AC is still not cooling properly, it may be time to call a professional. An HVAC expert can diagnose more complex issues and ensure your system is running optimally. From checking for electrical problems to performing thorough maintenance, a qualified technician can provide the peace of mind you need.
