Why Has My Furnace Stopped Working in the Texas Hill Country?

When the chilly Texas Hill Country nights set in, the last thing you want is for your furnace to stop working. Understanding the common reasons behind furnace failures can help you troubleshoot minor issues and know when to call in the pros.

Common Reasons Your Furnace Might Stop Working

Thermostat Issues

One of the most common reasons for furnace failure is a malfunctioning thermostat. Ensure your thermostat is set to 'heat' and check its battery if it's not hardwired. Sometimes, a simple reset can solve the issue.

Clogged Air Filters

Clogged air filters can restrict airflow, causing your furnace to overheat and shut down. Regularly inspect and replace filters to maintain efficient operation.

Pilot Light or Ignition Problems

Older furnaces with pilot lights may have a blown-out light. Newer models might face ignition issues. If you're uncomfortable relighting the pilot, contact a local HVAC technician for help.

DIY Fixes and Maintenance Tips

  • Check power supply and circuit breaker.
  • Inspect and replace air filters every 1-3 months.
  • Ensure vents are open and unblocked.
  • Test the thermostat by setting it higher than the current room temperature.

When to Call a Professional

If you've tried the basic troubleshooting steps and your furnace still isn't working, it's time to contact a professional. Residents in the Texas Hill Country can rely on local HVAC experts who understand the unique challenges of our climate. Whether it's a complex repair or routine maintenance, a professional can ensure your furnace is ready to keep your home comfortable all season long.
