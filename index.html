HVAC service for Hill Country homes

Limestone hills, long cooling seasons, and sudden cold snaps are hard on equipment that was sized for a milder climate. We take repair, maintenance, and replacement calls across the Kerrville area.

Emergency AC repair

No cool air, a frozen coil, a breaker that won’t stay on, or a system that short-cycles through a Hill Country afternoon.

Furnace & heating repair

Heat that won’t start, a furnace that blows cold, or a heat pump struggling on the first cold night of the season.

HVAC maintenance

Filter, coil, drain, and safety checks before summer peak and before the first freeze, so a small issue doesn’t become a no-cool call.

AC system installation

Replacement cooling equipment sized for the house, not a rule-of-thumb tonnage that leaves upstairs rooms warm.

Heating system installation

Furnace and heat-pump replacements when the equipment is old enough that another repair is no longer the right call.

Indoor air quality

Musty supply air, a clogged drain, or filtration questions after a wet spring in a closed-up house.

Ductless mini-splits

A room, garage apartment, or addition that the main system never quite reaches.

Questions we hear first

Do you cover Kerrville, Fredericksburg, and Boerne?

Yes. Those three, plus Comfort, Ingram, Center Point, Bandera, Hunt, Leakey, Rocksprings, and Mason. Call +1 (830) 307-7334 if you aren’t sure your address is in range.

Is this an emergency number?

Yes. Use +1 (830) 307-7334 for no-cool and no-heat emergencies any time. Scheduled estimates are handled Monday–Friday 7am–6pm and Saturday 8am–2pm.

What should I try before calling about an AC that isn’t cooling?

Check the thermostat is set to cool and below the room temperature, see whether the breaker has tripped, and look at the air filter. If the outdoor unit is iced over or silent, stop there and call +1 (830) 307-7334. More homeowner checks are on the HVAC blog.

How do I ask for an estimate?

Use the form on this page or call +1 (830) 307-7334. Include the town, whether it’s cooling or heat, and whether the system is running at all.
